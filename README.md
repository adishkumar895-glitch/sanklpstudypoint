A modern, responsive, and mobile-friendly website created for **Sanklap Study Point**, guided by **Rupesh Kumar Tiwari (Tiwari Baba)** in Bettiah, Bihar.

---

- **Header & Announcement Marquee:**

  - Direct call links to `9631120116` and `7857023185`.
- **Hero Section:**
  - Engaging headline, institute branding, and quick contact action buttons.

  - Floating WhatsApp chat and phone call buttons for instant mobile inquiries.
- **Footer:**
  - Full details, center address, and `"Created with ❤️ by AD"`.
